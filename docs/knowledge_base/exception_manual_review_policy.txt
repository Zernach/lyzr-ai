EXCEPTION AND MANUAL REVIEW POLICY

Document owner: Credit Risk and Compliance
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
This policy defines when an application must be escalated for manual review and
how policy exceptions are documented and approved.

2. Manual review principle
Manual review is required whenever the application falls outside standard policy,
contains incomplete information, or raises fairness, fraud, or interpretation
concerns that should not be resolved automatically.

3. Required manual review triggers
Escalate when any of the following occur:
- Credit score below 580
- DTI above 45 percent
- LTV above the standard cap for the vehicle class
- Income verification is partial or unavailable
- Bankruptcy, repossession, or severe recent delinquency requires judgment
- Application data is contradictory or suspicious
- Vehicle collateral is unusual, older, high-mileage, or otherwise non-standard
- Any fair lending concern exists
- AI output is vague, conflicting, or unsupported by retrieved policy

4. Permitted exception categories
Exceptions may be considered only for:
- Slightly above-policy DTI with strong compensating factors
- Slightly above-policy LTV with larger down payment or lower loss exposure
- Limited credit history offset by stable income and reserves
- Term adjustment, loan amount reduction, or down payment increase

5. Prohibited exceptions
Do not approve exceptions that:
- Rely on protected traits or proxy attributes
- Ignore inability to verify identity or income
- Override fraud concerns without resolution
- Depend on unsupported collateral values

6. Required exception memo fields
Every exception request must document:
- Policy rule exceeded
- Amount of variance from policy
- Compensating factors
- Residual risks
- Specific approval or denial decision
- Reviewer name and approval timestamp

7. AI system behavior
If a case meets any manual review trigger, the AI must not present the result as
final approval. It must state:
- "Manual review required"
- Which policy trigger caused escalation
- What information or approval is still needed

8. Approval authority
- Frontline automated or assisted workflow: may recommend only
- Senior underwriter: may approve standard manual review decisions
- Credit manager or delegated authority: required for formal policy exceptions

9. Monitoring
Exception volume and override patterns must be monitored for:
- Risk drift
- Inconsistent treatment
- Weak controls
- Possible fair lending concerns
