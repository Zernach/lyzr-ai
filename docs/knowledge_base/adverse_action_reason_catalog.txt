ADVERSE ACTION REASON CATALOG

Document owner: Compliance Operations
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
This catalog provides standardized, specific, and policy-grounded reasons for
declines, counteroffers, and other adverse or less favorable credit outcomes.

2. Usage rules
- Use the most specific reasons supported by verified facts
- Do not use reasons that are not documented in the file
- Do not use more reasons than necessary; one to four reasons is typical
- If the file is incomplete, use a verification-related reason instead of
  guessing at a credit weakness

3. Reason codes

AA-101
Title: Credit score below minimum threshold
Use when: Verified score is below the applicable program cutoff

AA-102
Title: Insufficient credit history
Use when: File lacks enough tradeline depth or history to support approval

AA-103
Title: Serious delinquency or derogatory credit history
Use when: Recent charge-off, collections severity, major delinquency, or similar

AA-104
Title: Recent repossession or auto-loan default history
Use when: Prior auto-loan performance materially increases collateral risk

AA-105
Title: Excessive debt obligations relative to income
Use when: DTI exceeds policy limits

AA-106
Title: Insufficient verified income for requested obligation
Use when: Verified income does not support payment affordability

AA-107
Title: Income could not be verified
Use when: Required documentation is missing, incomplete, or inconsistent

AA-108
Title: Employment or income stability is insufficient
Use when: Income history is too limited or unstable under policy

AA-109
Title: Loan-to-value ratio exceeds policy limit
Use when: Financed amount exceeds allowed share of vehicle value

AA-110
Title: Vehicle collateral does not meet program guidelines
Use when: Vehicle age, mileage, title status, or condition is ineligible

AA-111
Title: Requested term exceeds policy maximum
Use when: Loan term is outside program limits and no exception is approved

AA-112
Title: Required applicant information could not be verified
Use when: Identity, residence, or other required facts remain unverified

AA-113
Title: Material inconsistency in application information
Use when: Submitted data conflicts with documents or verified records

AA-114
Title: Payment affordability is outside policy tolerance
Use when: Payment-to-income or residual affordability fails program limits

AA-115
Title: Manual review required before final decision
Use when: Application is not ready for approval or decline without escalation

4. Counteroffer-oriented reasons
These reasons support less favorable terms instead of outright denial:

CO-201
Title: Lower loan amount required to meet loan-to-value policy

CO-202
Title: Larger down payment required to meet underwriting standards

CO-203
Title: Shorter loan term required to meet affordability policy

CO-204
Title: Qualified co-borrower or co-signer required

5. Reason selection examples
Example A:
- AA-105 Excessive debt obligations relative to income
- AA-109 Loan-to-value ratio exceeds policy limit

Example B:
- AA-107 Income could not be verified

Example C:
- CO-202 Larger down payment required to meet underwriting standards
- CO-203 Shorter loan term required to meet affordability policy

6. Prohibited reasons
Never use:
- Personal profile concerns
- Neighborhood risk
- Customer fit
- AI confidence score
- Protected trait or proxy references
