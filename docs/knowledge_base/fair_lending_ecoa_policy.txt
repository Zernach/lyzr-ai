FAIR LENDING AND ECOA POLICY

Document owner: Compliance
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
This policy defines the fair lending controls that apply to all auto loan
recommendations, underwriting workflows, and AI-assisted decision support.

2. Core rule
Credit decisions and recommendations must be based only on legitimate
creditworthiness, affordability, collateral, fraud, and verification factors.

3. Prohibited considerations
Do not consider any protected characteristic or direct proxy for a protected
characteristic, including:
- Race
- Color
- Religion
- National origin
- Sex
- Marital status
- Age, except where permitted by law
- Receipt of public assistance
- Good-faith exercise of rights under consumer credit laws

4. AI system restrictions
AI systems must not:
- Make final legal credit decisions without approved governance
- Use protected traits in prompts, features, or outputs
- Infer protected traits from names, photos, language, geolocation, schools,
  browsing behavior, social profile data, or other proxy signals
- Produce vague recommendations such as "bad profile" or "high risk person"
- Override written policy without documented exception approval

5. Acceptable underwriting inputs
The following categories may be used when verified and relevant:
- Credit score and tradeline history
- Verified income and debt obligations
- Loan amount, term, and payment affordability
- Vehicle value, age, mileage, and condition
- Identity and fraud checks
- Prior repayment performance where legally permissible

6. Consistency requirement
Similarly situated applicants must be treated consistently. If two applications
have materially similar credit and affordability facts, they should produce the
same recommendation category unless a documented policy exception applies.

7. Adverse action specificity
If an application is declined, counteroffered, or otherwise receives less
favorable terms, the explanation must use specific policy-based reasons.
Examples:
- Excessive debt obligations relative to income
- Credit score below minimum program threshold
- Loan-to-value exceeds policy limit
- Income could not be verified

Do not use vague explanations such as:
- Poor profile
- Risk concerns
- Failed AI review
- Not a fit

8. Manual review requirement
Escalate to manual review if there is any concern that:
- A recommendation may be influenced by a proxy for a protected trait
- The reason code is not sufficiently specific
- The facts are incomplete or contradictory
- The output is inconsistent with similar cases

9. Audit requirements
Every recommendation must retain:
- Applicant facts actually used
- Policy sections retrieved or applied
- Recommendation category
- Specific reason codes if adverse action is possible
- Identity of reviewer or system that produced the recommendation

10. Safe language for AI outputs
Preferred phrasing:
- "Based on verified financial information and retrieved underwriting policy..."
- "Manual review is required because income verification is incomplete."
- "The recommendation does not rely on protected characteristics."

Prohibited phrasing:
- "The applicant seems unreliable."
- "The neighborhood appears risky."
- "The applicant does not fit our customer profile."

11. Escalation
Any suspected disparate treatment, use of proxy data, or unexplained model
behavior must be escalated to Compliance before a final action is taken.
