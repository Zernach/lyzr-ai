AUDIT CHECKLIST

Document owner: Risk Governance
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

Purpose:
Use this checklist to confirm that every underwriting recommendation is
traceable, explainable, and supported by policy and verified applicant data.

Checklist:

1. Applicant facts
- Identity status documented
- Requested loan amount documented
- Requested term documented
- Vehicle details documented
- Credit score documented with source and date
- Income documents listed
- Monthly debt obligations listed

2. Calculations
- DTI calculated and recorded
- LTV calculated and recorded
- Payment affordability assessed

3. Policy retrieval and application
- Underwriting policy section identified
- Fair lending policy checked
- Vehicle valuation policy applied when relevant
- Income verification rules applied when relevant
- Exception policy checked when relevant

4. Recommendation quality
- Recommendation category stated
- Supporting factors stated
- Missing information stated
- Manual review requirement clearly stated when triggered
- Output does not claim to be a final legal decision unless approved workflow permits

5. Fair lending controls
- No protected trait used
- No prohibited proxy reasoning present
- Recommendation language is specific and neutral
- Similar-case consistency concern noted if applicable

6. Adverse action readiness
- Specific adverse action or counteroffer reasons selected if needed
- Reasons are supported by verified facts
- Reasons are clear and not vague

7. Governance
- System or reviewer identity captured
- Timestamp captured
- Exception approval captured if applicable
- Audit trail is complete enough for later review

Pass condition:
The file is audit-ready only when each applicable item above is complete and the
recommendation can be explained from retrieved policy plus verified facts alone.
