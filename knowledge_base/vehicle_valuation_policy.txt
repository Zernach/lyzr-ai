VEHICLE VALUATION POLICY

Document owner: Collateral Risk
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
This policy defines how vehicle value is determined for loan-to-value analysis
and collateral eligibility decisions.

2. Approved valuation sources
Use one of the following approved valuation sources:
- Dealer invoice or manufacturer suggested retail price for new vehicles
- Approved third-party retail valuation guide for used vehicles
- Internal collateral valuation service if approved by Risk

If multiple values are available, use the lowest reasonable supported value
unless a documented exception is approved.

3. Value used for LTV
For LTV calculations, use the lesser of:
- Purchase price
- Approved market value from valuation source

4. Included and excluded amounts
Include in financed amount:
- Base vehicle amount
- Approved taxes and registration fees
- Approved service contracts if program permits

Exclude from vehicle value:
- Negative equity from trade-in rollover
- Add-on products not eligible under program rules
- Unsupported dealer markup

5. Vehicle eligibility guidelines
Standard eligibility:
- Clean title
- No salvage, rebuilt, flood, or lemon-law branded title
- Personal-use passenger vehicle or light truck
- Mileage and age within program limits

Manual review required:
- Commercial-use vehicle
- Specialty or exotic vehicle
- Older high-mileage collateral
- Modified vehicle with uncertain resale value

6. Mileage and age guidance
- New vehicle: standard policy applies
- Used vehicle up to 5 model years old and under 75,000 miles: standard policy
- Used vehicle 6 to 8 model years old or 75,000 to 120,000 miles: tighter LTV
- Older than 8 model years or over 120,000 miles: manual review only

7. LTV interpretation
- Low risk: 90 percent or less
- Standard risk: over 90 percent to 100 percent
- Elevated risk: over 100 percent to policy cap
- Out of policy: above policy cap

8. Required output for AI or assisted review
Every valuation assessment must include:
- Valuation source used
- Value amount used for LTV
- Financed amount used for LTV
- Calculated LTV
- Any collateral exception or eligibility concern

9. Escalation triggers
Escalate to manual review when:
- Valuation sources materially conflict
- Dealer price appears inflated relative to market value
- Title status is unclear
- Vehicle is outside standard age, mileage, or condition tolerance

10. Control reminder
Vehicle value policy supports collateral risk review only. It must not be used
as a proxy for applicant demographics or neighborhood characteristics.
