AUTO LOAN UNDERWRITING POLICY

Document owner: Credit Risk
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
This policy defines the baseline underwriting standards for consumer auto loans.
It is intended for use by underwriters, rules engines, and AI support systems that
produce preliminary recommendations. It does not authorize fully automated final
credit decisions without an approved human or rules-engine checkpoint.

2. Decision categories
Applications must be classified into one of the following categories:
- Preliminary approve
- Conditional approve
- Manual review
- Decline candidate

3. Core underwriting factors
Every recommendation must consider all of the following:
- Credit score
- Debt-to-income ratio (DTI)
- Loan-to-value ratio (LTV)
- Verified gross monthly income
- Employment or income stability
- Recent delinquencies or derogatory events
- Requested term and payment affordability
- Vehicle eligibility and valuation

4. Credit score bands
Use score bands as a starting point only. They are not automatic decisions.

Band A: 740 and above
- Risk level: low
- Eligible for standard approval review
- Best pricing and longest available terms may be considered

Band B: 670 to 739
- Risk level: moderate
- Eligible for standard approval review

Band C: 580 to 669
- Risk level: elevated
- Requires tighter DTI, lower LTV, or compensating factors
- Conditional approval or manual review is common

Band D: below 580
- Risk level: high
- Requires manual review before any approval recommendation
- Decline is likely unless strong compensating factors exist

5. Debt-to-income limits
DTI is calculated as total monthly debt obligations divided by verified gross monthly income.

Maximum DTI by recommendation path:
- Preliminary approve: 40 percent
- Conditional approve: 45 percent
- Manual review threshold: over 45 percent and up to 50 percent
- Decline candidate: over 50 percent unless a documented exception is approved

6. Loan-to-value limits
LTV is calculated as total financed amount divided by approved vehicle value.

Maximum LTV by vehicle class:
- New vehicle: 115 percent
- Used vehicle up to 5 model years old: 110 percent
- Used vehicle 6 to 8 model years old: 100 percent
- Used vehicle older than 8 model years: manual review only

If score band is C or D, reduce the maximum LTV by 5 percentage points.

7. Income and affordability requirements
- Minimum verified gross monthly income: 1,800 dollars
- Payment-to-income target: 15 percent or less of gross monthly income
- Payment-to-income above 20 percent requires manual review
- Unverified income cannot be used for final recommendation

8. Term limits
- New vehicle: up to 72 months
- Used vehicle up to 5 model years old: up to 60 months
- Used vehicle older than 5 model years: up to 48 months
- Terms above these limits require manual review and documented justification

9. Compensating factors
Compensating factors may support conditional approval or manual review outcomes:
- Down payment of 15 percent or more
- Verified stable employment or income history of at least 12 months
- Significant liquid reserves
- Strong recent payment history
- Qualified co-borrower or co-signer
- Lower-than-requested loan amount accepted by applicant

10. Automatic manual review triggers
Escalate to manual review if any of the following are true:
- Credit score below 580
- DTI above 45 percent
- LTV above standard policy limits
- Income cannot be fully verified
- Prior repossession within 24 months
- Bankruptcy discharged within 24 months
- Thin credit file with limited tradeline depth
- Vehicle outside standard age or mileage policy

11. Decline candidate triggers
An application is a decline candidate when one or more of the following apply and
no approved exception exists:
- DTI above 50 percent
- Material misrepresentation or inconsistent application data
- Inability to verify identity, income, or residence
- Vehicle collateral does not meet eligibility standards
- Payment affordability is clearly unsustainable
- Multiple severe recent delinquencies or active repossession exposure

12. Output requirements for AI or assisted review
Any AI-generated recommendation must include:
- Recommendation category
- Relevant score band
- DTI calculation and result
- LTV calculation and result
- Income verification status
- Missing information
- Whether manual review is required
- Specific policy-based reasons for the recommendation

13. Fair lending reminder
Protected characteristics and prohibited proxy variables must never be used in
recommendation logic. See the fair lending and ECOA policy for complete rules.
