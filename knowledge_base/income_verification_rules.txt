INCOME VERIFICATION RULES

Document owner: Operations
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for knowledge base use

1. Purpose
These rules define acceptable income documentation and how verified income may be
used in underwriting recommendations.

2. General rule
Income must be stable, recurring where required, and supported by current
documentation. If income cannot be verified, it cannot be used for approval.

3. Standard salaried or hourly employee
Preferred documentation:
- Most recent 30 days of pay stubs
- Most recent W-2
- Employer verification when needed

Use current gross monthly income unless there is evidence of declining hours,
temporary employment, or inconsistent pay.

4. Self-employed applicant
Required documentation:
- Two years of personal tax returns
- Two years of business tax returns if applicable
- Year-to-date profit and loss statement when needed
- Recent business bank statements if required for validation

Use averaged income unless current-year evidence supports a lower amount.

5. Commission, bonus, or overtime income
Use only if:
- Received consistently
- Documented with history, typically 12 months or more
- Reasonably expected to continue

If history is inconsistent, exclude the variable component or escalate.

6. Public benefits, retirement, or fixed non-employment income
May be considered if legally permissible and documented through award letters,
benefit statements, pension statements, or deposit history.

Do not treat receipt of public assistance as a negative factor. Only assess
whether the income is stable, documentable, and sufficient.

7. Bank statement support
Bank statements may support income consistency but do not replace primary income
documentation unless an approved alternative documentation path exists.

8. Red flags requiring manual review
- Large unexplained income changes
- Altered or inconsistent documents
- Claimed income materially exceeds documented deposits
- Temporary job status with insufficient history
- Cash income without reliable supporting records

9. Documentation recency
- Pay stubs should generally be within the last 30 days
- Bank statements should generally be within the last 60 days
- Tax returns should be the two most recent filed years when required

10. Verification outcomes
Use one of the following statuses:
- Fully verified
- Partially verified
- Unable to verify

Applications with partially verified or unable-to-verify income require either
additional documents or manual review before approval.

11. AI output requirements
Any recommendation referencing income must state:
- Income source type
- Documents reviewed
- Verification status
- Verified monthly income used in calculations
- Any missing documents
